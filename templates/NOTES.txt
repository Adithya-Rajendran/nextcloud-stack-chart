VERIFY CLUSTER ASSUMPTIONS
--------------------------
- networkPolicy.inClusterCidrs (currently {{ .Values.networkPolicy.inClusterCidrs }})
  must cover BOTH the pod CIDR and the Service CIDR so in-cluster traffic stays
  on the explicit per-Service rules. The RFC1918 default fits most clusters;
  narrow it for tighter egress control. Find both with:
    kubectl get nodes -o jsonpath='{.items[*].spec.podCIDR}'
    kubectl cluster-info dump | grep -m1 service-cluster-ip-range
- NetworkPolicies only enforce if your CNI supports them (Cilium, Calico, …).
